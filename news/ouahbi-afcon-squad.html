Ouahbi Set to Name Morocco Squad for 2027 AFCON Qualifiers Amid Sweeping Changes

📅 2026-09-18✍️ MatchPulse Team
🦁

National coach Mohamed Ouahbi is set to announce his new Morocco squad ahead of the start of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Gabon on 25 September, followed by Lesotho four days later.

According to press reports, the squad is expected to be without at least six players who featured at the 2026 World Cup, three of them through injury: Chadi Riad (cruciate ligament tear), Youssef Belammari (a similar injury), and Ayoub El Kaabi (broken leg).

Marouane Saâdane, Anas Salah-Eddine and Redouane Halhal are also expected to be left out through a lack of fitness, while new faces are set to include Younes Ben Taleb (Eintracht Frankfurt), who caught the eye with a hat-trick in front of Ouahbi himself on 29 August, Yasser Zbirri (Racing Santander), Othmane Maamma (Watford), and Omar El Hilali (Espanyol).

Ouahbi took charge of the Atlas Lions last March, succeeding Walid Regragui, and led the team to the 2026 World Cup quarter-finals before the defeat to France.

The 2027 AFCON qualifiers mark the first step in a project to build a new generation targeting continental glory and a strong run at the 2030 World Cup, which will be held in Morocco.
