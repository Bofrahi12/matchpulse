City and Arsenal Share Top Spot as Haaland Keeps Chasing the Goals

📅 2026-09-13✍️ MatchPulse Team
🏴

Manchester City and Arsenal share the Premier League lead on 12 points each after 5 rounds of the 2026-27 season, in a start that confirms the title race will be fiercely contested.

City hold the goal-difference advantage (+6) after 4 straight wins, while Arsenal have played an extra game (4 wins and a defeat).

The pleasant surprise has been Brighton, third on 10 points with the strongest attack (16 goals), followed by Brentford and Everton on nine points each.

In the scoring charts, Erling Haaland leads the way with 4 goals, ahead of a chasing pack on three goals, most notably João Pedro (Chelsea), Bukayo Saka (Arsenal) and Bruno Fernandes (Manchester United).

At the other end, Tottenham are struggling in 18th place on just two points, while newly promoted Coventry City are still without a point after 4 straight defeats.
