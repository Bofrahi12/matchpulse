Raphinha: Barcelona's Machine Leading Europe's Scoring Charts Unchallenged

📅 2026-09-15✍️ MatchPulse Team
🇧🇷

Brazilian Raphinha continues to enjoy an exceptional season by every measure, having raised his tally to 12 goals in 7 La Liga matches and leading the scoring charts across Europe's major leagues by a comfortable margin.

According to newspaper "Mundo Deportivo", Barcelona's captain (29) also leads the goal-contribution charts in Europe's top five leagues, having scored in his team's first five matches before setting up two goals in the 4-2 win over Levante.

Manager Hansi Flick is using Raphinha as an out-and-out striker this season, a role that has brought out the best in the player, who also scored twice in the 5-1 win over Feyenoord.

His closest challengers in La Liga are teammate Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappé (7 goals each), followed by Sergio Camello (Rayo Vallecano) on the same tally, while Erling Haaland leads the Premier League scorers with 4 goals and Donyell Malen leads Serie A with 5.

Barcelona's frightening numbers do not stop at Raphinha: the team have won 7 out of 7 matches and scored 31 goals, the strongest start in Europe this season.
