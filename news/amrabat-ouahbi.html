Amrabat: No Dispute with Ouahbi… But No One's Place in the National Team Is Guaranteed

📅 2026-09-17✍️ MatchPulse Team
🎙️

National coach Mohamed Ouahbi has denied any personal dispute with Sofyan Amrabat, after the Ajax Amsterdam midfielder announced earlier this month that he would not play under Ouahbi, while confirming that he has not retired from international football.

Ouahbi told Reuters after announcing his first squad since the 2026 World Cup quarter-final exit: "Sofyan has given a lot to the national team and we must remember that… but there is no conflict between us; it is only about playing minutes."

He added: "In football, the past earns you respect, but it earns you no privileges. Our vision is based on merit and current fitness. The door is not closed to anyone, but no one is guaranteed a place."

Amrabat (30) was a key figure in the team that reached the 2022 World Cup semi-finals under Walid Regragui, but his role has diminished since Ouahbi took charge in March, starting just one match at the 2026 World Cup amid fierce competition, most notably from rising talent Ayoub Bouaddi of Manchester City.
