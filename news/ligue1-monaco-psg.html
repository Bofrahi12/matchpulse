Monaco Top Ligue 1 as Champions PSG Stumble in a Disappointing Start

📅 2026-09-12✍️ MatchPulse Team
🇫🇷

Monaco continued their strong start in Ligue 1 and climbed to the top on 13 points from 5 matches (4 wins and a draw), ahead of Lyon and surprise package Paris FC (11 points each).

The big surprise has been the stuttering start of reigning champions Paris Saint-Germain, who sit in 10th place on just five points from 4 matches (a win, two draws and a defeat), despite their big European triumph last season.

Lille sit fourth on 10 points under new manager Davide Ancelotti — son of Carlo Ancelotti — who took charge last June, strengthening his squad with Japanese striker Ayase Ueda, last season's top scorer in the Dutch league.

In the scoring charts, Amine Gouiri (Marseille), Kamory Doumbia (Brest) and Lassine Sinayoko (Paris FC) share the lead with four goals each.
