About us

MatchPulse is an independent football site in English. We launched in September 2026 with one goal: to give fans in the USA, the UK and Europe a fast, reliable way to follow everything that matters in the beautiful game.

What we offer

  • ⚽ Live scores and today’s fixtures from the biggest competitions.
  • 📊 Updated league tables for Europe’s five major leagues and the Moroccan Botola Pro.
  • 📰 Daily news in a clear, direct style, with special attention to the Morocco national team and the Atlas Lions.
  • 👟 Top-scorer races and simple stats for every league.

Our data sources

We rely on trusted, public sports sources to update results and tables, and we name the source of every figure and the date it was checked. We never present rumours as facts: anything unconfirmed is clearly labelled as such.

Important note

MatchPulse does not stream matches and provides no illegal viewing links. We respect broadcast rights and ask our readers to watch matches through the official licensed channels in their countries.
